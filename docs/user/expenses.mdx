---
title: Track expenses and settle up
description: Record shared spending, split costs, and understand who owes whom.
---

## Record spending when it happens

Add the amount, currency, payer, participants, category, and a short
description. Recording expenses during the trip reduces missing context later.

## Check the split

![The OpenTrip budget view with shared expenses and settlement balances](/assets/screenshots/pc-budget.jpg)

Before saving, confirm that every person who benefited is included and that the
payer is correct. Use a custom split when equal shares do not match the real
agreement.

## Settle after review

Review the expense list together before using the settlement summary. Currency
conversion helps compare balances, but the group should still agree on the
rate and final payment method.
