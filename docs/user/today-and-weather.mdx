## Open Today

From the home hub, open **Today**. Use it between journeys — when you are not
inside a single trip planner but still want local context.

  src="/assets/demos/today.mp4"

## Set your current place

Choose a city or region for Today. OpenTrip remembers the selection for your
account on this device and uses it for local weather. Prefer naming the place
yourself instead of relying on background location permission.

## Check the weather

Today shows current conditions for the place you selected. Inside a trip, day
planning can also use weather context so the group sees whether outdoor stops
still make sense.

Weather is advisory. Confirm opening hours, alerts, and travel advisories from
primary sources before you change locked bookings.

## Prepare or reflect

From Today you can:

- jump into an upcoming trip to finish the plan;
- capture a moment in a travelogue;
- revisit a trip that recently ended.

Travelogue capture from Today or a stop is covered in
[Travelogues](./travelogues.mdx).
