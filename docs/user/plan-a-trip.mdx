## Work from the trip overview

The planner brings the map and schedule together. Use the overview to understand
the whole route, then open a day when you need detailed changes.

Planner modes:

| Mode | Use it to |
| --- | --- |
| **Map** | See numbered stops and day routes; select a stop for details |
| **Schedule** | Insert, reorder, and edit stops day by day |
| **Reservations** | Keep confirmed bookings next to the itinerary |
| **Budget** | Record expenses and review settlement |

## Manage days

On the schedule board you can:

- append a new day;
- update day metadata (title or notes when available);
- reorder days when the sequence changes;
- delete a day you no longer need (remaining days renumber).

Keep transport between cities as explicit stops or reservations so the map and
timeline tell the same story.

## Add and edit stops

For each stop, set:

- place name and location;
- expected time;
- category (for example Stay, Sight, Food, Transport);
- notes the group will need on the day.

Treat uncertain ideas as proposals and confirmed bookings as commitments. When
a day becomes crowded, move or remove stops instead of stacking overlapping
times.

## Stop detail, notes, and media

Open a stop to edit timing, category, Markdown notes, and attached images.
Upload photos that help the group recognize a place or entrance. Keep passport
scans and payment details out of notes — store only what the group needs to
travel.

![Stop detail with notes and media in the OpenTrip planner](/assets/screenshots/pc-stop-detail.jpg)

## Rename the trip

Use the trip header rename control when the destination or nickname changes.
Editors and the owner can rename; viewers cannot.
