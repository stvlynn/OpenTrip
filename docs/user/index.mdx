OpenTrip keeps the map, itinerary, reservations, shared costs, and trip
conversation in one place. This guide follows the same order as a real trip:
get started, build the plan, invite your group, travel together, and settle up.

![The OpenTrip product homepage showing the trip planning experience](/assets/screenshots/product-home.png)

## Choose your next step

- **New to OpenTrip?** Start with [Getting started](./getting-started.mdx).
- **Already have a trip?** Learn how to [plan the itinerary](./plan-a-trip.mdx).
- **Traveling with a group?** Review [collaboration](./collaborate.mdx) and
  [expenses](./expenses.mdx).
- **Want help from AI?** Meet the [AI trip companion](./ai-companion.mdx).

## How this guide is organized

User documentation describes outcomes and workflows rather than internal
implementation. Developer-facing architecture, APIs, and operational runbooks
live under **Developer docs**, available from the perspective switcher in the
navigation.
