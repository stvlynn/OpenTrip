description: Manage sign-in methods, password recovery, profile settings, and 2FA.

## Sign-in options

OpenTrip supports email and password, Google, and WeChat where configured.
Available providers can vary by deployment.

## Recover or change a password

From the sign-in screen, choose **Forgot password?** and complete the email
verification flow. Signed-in users can change or establish a password from
profile settings.

## Add two-factor authentication

Enable 2FA from security settings, save backup codes somewhere outside
OpenTrip, and verify that your authenticator works before leaving the setup
screen.

## Protect invitations and sessions

Treat trip invites like private links. Sign out on shared devices and revoke
access when a device or invite may have been exposed.
