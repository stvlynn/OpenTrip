## Create or access your account

Open [OpenTrip](https://opentrip.im), choose **Start planning**, and continue
with email, Google, or WeChat. Available providers depend on how the deployment
is configured.

  poster="/assets/screenshots/sign-in.png"
/>

| Method | What to expect |
| --- | --- |
| Email + password | Sign-up may require an email OTP before the first password sign-in |
| Google | OAuth when the Google provider is enabled |
| WeChat | Website QR login and/or the WeChat Mini Program shell when configured |

Password recovery and two-factor authentication are covered in
[Account and security](./account-and-security.mdx).

## Browse the trips home

After sign-in you land on the trips library. Each card shows status (for
example planning, active, or settled), dates, cover art, and member avatars.

  src="/assets/demos/trips.mp4"

From the home hub you can also open:

- **Today** — a current place, weather, and quick actions between journeys
- **Travelogues** — personal moment capture (device-local preview today)

On narrow screens those destinations appear in the bottom navigation. See
[Today and weather](./today-and-weather.mdx), [Travelogues](./travelogues.mdx),
and [WeChat and mobile](./wechat-and-mobile.mdx).

## Create a trip

1. Choose **Create trip**.
2. Describe the destination and choose travel dates.
3. Optionally note party size or a planned budget. These fields do **not**
   pre-create members or expenses.
4. Review the suggested cover (Unsplash when a destination is set), then create.

OpenTrip navigates into the planner. On first open you may see a one-shot
AI-suggested draft; review it before treating anything as confirmed.

## Open an existing trip

Select a trip card to open the planner. Desktop uses a sidebar plus Map /
Schedule / Reservations / Budget tabs. Mobile uses a bottom tab bar and sheets
for itinerary, stop detail, and the AI companion.

## Before inviting companions

Add enough context for the group to recognize the plan:

- a clear trip name;
- correct dates and destination;
- the first few confirmed reservations;
- notes for decisions that are still open.
