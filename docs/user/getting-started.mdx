## Create or access your account

Open [OpenTrip](https://opentrip.im), choose **Start planning**, and continue
with email, Google, or WeChat. Email sign-up may require verification before
the first password sign-in.

![The real OpenTrip sign-in page with email, Google, and WeChat options](/assets/screenshots/sign-in.png)

## Create your first trip

![The OpenTrip trip library on desktop with active, planned, and settled trips](/assets/screenshots/pc-trips.jpg)

1. From the home screen, choose **Create trip**.
2. Describe where you are going and choose the travel dates.
3. Review the suggested cover and trip details.
4. Create the trip, then start adding days, stops, and reservations.

## Before inviting companions

Add enough context for the group to recognize the plan:

- a clear trip name;
- correct dates and destination;
- the first few confirmed reservations;
- notes for decisions that are still open.

Next, [build the itinerary](./plan-a-trip.mdx) or
[invite your travel group](./collaborate.mdx).
