## Add a reservation

1. Open the trip and choose **Reservations**.
2. Create a reservation and choose its type (lodging, transport, or activity).
3. Fill in the title, timing, and booking reference.
4. Add notes that matter on the day (check-in window, gate, meeting point).

Put booking references on the reservation itself rather than scattering them
across stop comments.

## Update or cancel

- Edit a reservation when times or references change.
- Cancel when the booking is no longer valid but you want a record of what
  happened.
- Delete only when the entry was created by mistake and should not remain in
  history.

OpenTrip tracks revisions on reservation updates so concurrent edits stay
consistent. If a save fails because someone else changed the same booking,
reload and re-apply your change.

## Keep itinerary and bookings aligned

Link mental models clearly:

- Stops describe **where you intend to be**.
- Reservations describe **what you already booked**.

A lodging stop and a lodging reservation often describe the same stay — keep
names and dates consistent so the map, schedule, and reservations board agree.

## Ask the companion for lodging ideas

The AI companion can search vacation rentals and propose options. Those
searches are research tools — adding a stay to the trip still requires your
approval, and recording a confirmation belongs on the reservations board. See
[AI companion](./ai-companion.mdx).

## On mobile

Reservations appear as one of the four planner tabs in the bottom navigation.
The same create and edit flows run inside the mobile shell.
