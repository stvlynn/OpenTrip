---
title: Collaborate with your group
description: Invite companions and keep trip decisions visible to everyone.
---

## Invite companions

Open the trip member controls, create an invite, and share it only with the
people joining the trip. Invite links grant access to the shared workspace, so
avoid posting them publicly.

## Make decisions in context

Keep notes and updates attached to the day, stop, reservation, or expense they
belong to. This makes the current plan easier to understand than a long group
chat history.

## Watch for live updates

When another member edits the trip, OpenTrip updates connected sessions. If a
change does not appear immediately, refresh before repeating the action.
