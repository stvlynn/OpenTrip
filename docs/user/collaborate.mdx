## Invite companions

1. Open the trip member / invite controls.
2. Create an invite and choose:
   - **access** — anyone with the link, or restricted to listed emails;
   - **role** — `editor` or `viewer` for people who join through this link;
   - **can invite** — whether new members may create further invites;
   - **expiry** — when the link stops working.
3. Share the link only with people joining the trip.

Invite links grant access to the shared workspace. Do not post them publicly.
Regenerate a link if it may have leaked.

## Vote on stops

Editors can toggle a vote on a stop to signal interest. Votes are trip-local —
use them to see which options the group prefers before locking the day.

## Comment in context

Add comments on a stop when the discussion belongs to that place. Mention a
teammate with `@Name` when they need to respond. Mention `@agent` when you want
the AI companion to answer in that stop’s thread.

Keep notes attached to the day, stop, reservation, or expense they belong to.
That is easier to revisit than a long free-form chat history.

## Watch live updates

When another member saves a change, connected sessions receive a realtime
update and refresh the affected parts of the trip. Member presence shows who is
currently in the planner.

If a change does not appear, refresh before repeating the same edit. Realtime
keeps the group aligned; it is not a substitute for agreeing on decisions in
comments or chat.
