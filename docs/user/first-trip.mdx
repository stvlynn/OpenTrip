---
title: Your first shared trip
description: A short lesson that takes you from sign-in to a shared itinerary with one companion.
---

This tutorial walks through one successful outcome: a two-person trip with a
destination, a couple of stops, a reservation, and an invite. Follow the steps
in order the first time; later you can jump to individual how-to guides.

## What you will have at the end

- An OpenTrip account
- One trip with dates and a cover
- At least two stops on the schedule
- One reservation
- A second person invited as an editor

## 1. Sign in

1. Open [OpenTrip](https://opentrip.im).
2. Choose **Start planning**.
3. Continue with email, Google, or WeChat (whichever your deployment offers).
4. If you used email, complete verification before the first password sign-in.

## 2. Create the trip

1. From the trips home, choose **Create trip**.
2. Enter a destination and travel dates. Optional fields (party size, planned
   budget) are hints for the group — they do not create members or expenses.
3. Review the suggested cover, then create the trip.

<DemoVideo
  src="/assets/demos/trips.mp4"
  caption="Open the trips home and continue into a shared trip"
  poster="/assets/screenshots/pc-trips.jpg"
/>

On first open, OpenTrip may offer an AI-suggested draft. Read it, edit anything
that looks wrong, and only send or apply changes you want on the shared plan.

## 3. Add the first stops

1. Open the **Schedule** tab.
2. Insert two stops on day one — for example a morning sight and a lunch stop.
3. For each stop, set a place name, approximate time, and category.
4. Switch to **Map** and confirm both stops appear on the route.

<DemoVideo
  src="/assets/demos/schedule.mp4"
  caption="Review timing and stop order on the daily schedule board"
  poster="/assets/screenshots/pc-schedule.jpg"
/>

## 4. Record one reservation

1. Open **Reservations**.
2. Add lodging, transport, or an activity you have already booked.
3. Include the booking reference and any check-in notes the group needs.

<DemoVideo
  src="/assets/demos/reservations.mp4"
  caption="Scan lodging, transport, and activity reservations beside the itinerary"
  poster="/assets/screenshots/pc-reservations.jpg"
/>

## 5. Invite a companion

1. Open the trip member / invite controls.
2. Create an invite with role **editor** so your companion can edit the plan.
3. Share the link privately — never post it publicly.

<DemoVideo
  src="/assets/demos/invite.mp4"
  caption="Create an invite link, copy it, and share it privately with your companion"
/>

When they accept, they appear in the member list and can edit the same trip.
Connected sessions refresh when someone else saves a change.
